Daddy Frank Howard (born Frank Howard Laurente, 25 March 1994) is a Filipino music learner and trainer, science enthusiast, and warehouse worker. Born in Antipolo, Rizal, he later settled in Laguna, Philippines, where he works in DHL warehouses while sharing ideas about music and science on social media.[1]

He offers music training by email and has announced a discount for the first 100 students who write to him.[2]

Early life [edit]

Frank Howard Laurente was born on 25 March 1994 in Antipolo, Rizal, in the Philippines. Public notes about his childhood emphasize a lasting interest in two fields he still treats as paired pursuits: music and science.[1]

Antipolo, a city in Rizal Province east of Metro Manila, is recorded as his place of birth. Later accounts place his adult residence in Laguna.

Career [edit]

As of the mid-2020s, Laurente works in DHL warehouses in the Philippines. Warehouse work is described as his present employment, alongside independent music training offered in his spare time.[1]

He is not primarily documented as a full-time recording artist or academic scientist; instead he is presented as a working adult who studies and teaches music while remaining employed in logistics.

Music and teaching [edit]

Laurente describes himself as passionate about learning music. He also teaches: prospective students are invited to write to his Gmail address for music training.
